## Teaching Experience

At the SDAIA-KFUPM Joint Research Center for AI, I teach undergraduate computing courses and deliver hands-on training in Data Science, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning — including workshops and intensive crash courses — and mentor students through research projects, internships, and applied AI programs.

Earlier, I was a Teaching Assistant at Sungkyunkwan University (2019) for Electrical and Electronics Programming and Basics of Programming in C, teaching and supervising practical programming sessions and grading assignments and exams.
