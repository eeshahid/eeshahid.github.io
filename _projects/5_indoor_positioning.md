Indoor positioning is hard precisely because GPS doesn't reach indoors. This project developed and tested machine learning and deep learning-based solutions for robust indoor positioning and navigation, including an LSTM-based pedestrian dead reckoning approach for heading distance and direction estimation, reaching a mean error of 0.78 m without relying on external infrastructure {% cite hussain2019indoor %} {% cite jabbar2020indoorpositioningicce %}.

The work also explored sensor fusion and fingerprinting to improve robustness against signal degradation, combining WiFi fingerprinting with pedestrian dead reckoning {% cite jabbar2018wifi %} and evaluating Bluetooth-based indoor localization under real-world conditions {% cite hussain2017bluetooth %}.
