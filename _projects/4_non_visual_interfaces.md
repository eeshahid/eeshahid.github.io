This line of work, carried out at Sungkyunkwan University's H-Lab, develops assistive technologies that translate visual information into non-visual channels for blind and visually impaired users.

Contributions include a reconfigurable tactile platform for non-visual information transmission and **ColorWatch**, a color-perceptual spatial tactile interface {% cite jabbar2021colorwatch %} {% cite lee2020colorwatchictc %}. On the multi-modal AI side, the **AI-Ekphrasis** framework uses transformer-based foundation models to retrieve fine-grained text descriptions for images, improving mean retrieval ranking to 8.2 versus a 15.5 baseline {% cite jabbar2022aiekphrasis %}. Related work on automatically generating art captions for visually impaired audiences received a Best Paper Award {% cite lee2021languageofgleam %}, and the tactile color-display device developed alongside this project was granted a Korean patent {% cite cho2020patent %}.
